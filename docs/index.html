Case study 03 / circuit verification

A truth table is only part of the specification.

The textbook XOR passes four unloaded points. A 10 kΩ load causes one high-level failure; another passing point draws significant current from an input. A separate latch selects different states after tiny initial perturbations.

Inspect the XOR

The intended behavior decides the verdict.

Loading, input-driver assumptions, voltage thresholds, timing, and state dependence must be declared. A circuit can pass sampled levels while violating another requirement.

The public examples reveal no optimized research architecture or search recipe. Five paired draws per corner in the robustness showcase demonstrate model sensitivity, not manufacturing yield.
